What we make

Woven womenswear is our core: shirts, blouses and dresses, from poplin and viscose to crepe, satin and linen. Other womenswear categories are developed with you case by case.

Two ways to work with us

Most brands start with the first and move to the second as the relationship settles. Both run on the same team, the same line and the same quality control.

Cut, make and trim

You supply fabric and trims, or have them delivered to us from your nominated mill. We prepare the pattern and samples, cut, sew, finish, pack and ship.

Delivery, as you need it

Garments leave finished, pressed and packed the way your warehouse receives them: on hangers in the trailer, or boxed and palletised. Road freight reaches the United Kingdom and Europe within 7–10 days.

Export documents and origin declarations are prepared by us. Routes and transit times are confirmed per order.
