Thirty years

Founded thirty years ago, the factory has made shirts, blouses and dresses for the suppliers of Europe's largest fashion retailers ever since. That work set the standard we hold today: audited lines, consistent quality, and seasons delivered on time.

Production remains in Bergama. Our commercial office in Manchester works with brands and sourcing agents across Europe.

The team

A compact, practical team that has produced together for years. Most supervisors and operators have been with the factory for more than a decade, so the experience on the line is collective, and a new style settles into production quickly.
