Third colour: replacing the blue oval of the original Martı Tekstil logo

The original sign is a navy oval with coral letters. In the new palette the letters become the clay accent (#C9573A). The oval needs a colour that is neither the ground nor the band, so it reads as a third element. Four candidates, each shown as the oval mark and next to the MARTI wordmark that the website uses.
