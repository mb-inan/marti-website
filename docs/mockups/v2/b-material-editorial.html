What we make

Woven womenswear is our core: shirts, blouses and dresses, from poplin and viscose to crepe, satin and linen. Other womenswear categories are developed with you case by case.

From tech pack to trailer

One team takes a style from your tech pack to a finished, packed garment ready for the road: pattern and samples, cutting, a sewing line specialised in wovens, finishing and dispatch, under one roof in İzmir.

Development

Cutting

Sewing
