What we make

Woven womenswear is our core: shirts, blouses and dresses, from poplin and viscose to crepe, satin and linen. Other womenswear categories are developed with you case by case.

Ways of working

Cut, make and trim

You supply fabric and trims, or have them delivered to us from your nominated mill. We prepare the pattern and samples, cut, sew, finish, pack and ship.

You bring
Tech pack, pattern or sample, fabric
We add
Pattern, grading, marker, samples, production, inspection, packing

Nominated fabric

You choose the mill; we place and manage the fabric order with Turkish mills we know, so one partner is responsible for the garment from fabric to delivery.

You bring
Tech pack, fabric choice, approvals
We add
Fabric purchase and handling, pattern, samples, production

From tech pack to trailer

Development

Pattern, grading, marker and samples prepared from your tech pack. Samples within 5–7 working days from fabric in hand.

Cutting

Sewing

Finishing and dispatch
